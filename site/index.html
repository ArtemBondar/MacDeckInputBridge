THE IDEA / 01

Your desk, less clutter.
More Steam Deck.

A docked Steam Deck doesn’t need another keyboard taking up space. MacDeck connects the keyboard on your Mac to your Deck over your local network. Switch between them with a shortcut whenever you need to type.

01 / KEYBOARD

A quick switch.

Send your Mac keyboard to the Deck and return it to your Mac with Control–Shift–F12. Your Mac menu bar shows where input is going.

02 / LAYOUTS

Familiar keys.

Match your active Mac layout to a configured Deck layout. For custom or ambiguous layouts, set a manual mapping.

03 / CLIPBOARD

Words can travel too.

Optionally share plain text in either direction during an active connection. Each direction is off by default.

IN REAL LIFE

A little easier to use.

Set up at your desk

Dock your Deck, open a browser, and type with the keyboard already on your Mac.

Switch when you need to type

Use Control–Shift–F12 to send your keyboard to the Deck, then use it again to return to your Mac.

Share text between devices

Enable clipboard sharing for either direction to copy text on one device and paste it on the other.

GET STARTED / 02

Two apps.
A few easy steps.

Install MacDeck on each device, pair them with the same secret, and enable Mac input on the Deck.

Read the setup guide ↗
  1. 01

    Install on your Mac

    Download the menu-bar app and grant Accessibility access. The current release requires a first-launch approval in macOS settings.

  2. 02

    Install on Steam Deck

    Switch to Desktop Mode, install the receiver, and complete the virtual keyboard permission setup.

  3. 03

    Pair and switch

    Copy the Deck’s IP address and pairing secret into the Mac app, then enable input on the Deck.

A NOTE ON PRIVACY / 03

Kept between
your devices.

The connection is authenticated and encrypted. Typed text and clipboard contents are not logged or stored. Use MacDeck on a trusted network and keep your pairing secret private.
