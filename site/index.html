MADE FOR MAC & STEAM DECK

Dock your Deck.
Keep your keyboard.

Use your MacBook keyboard to type on your Steam Deck in Desktop Mode. MacDeck sends keystrokes over your local network, and a shortcut switches the keyboard back to your Mac.

THE IDEA / 01

One keyboard for your Mac and Steam Deck

If your Deck is docked next to your MacBook, use the keyboard already in front of you to search in a browser, enter a command in Konsole, or edit a config file on the Deck.

01 / KEYBOARD

Switch with a shortcut

Press Control–Shift–F12 to send keystrokes to the Deck. Press it again to type on your Mac. You can also switch from the Mac menu bar, which shows where your keystrokes are going.

02 / LAYOUTS

Sync keyboard layouts

If you switch layouts on your Mac, MacDeck can switch to the matching layout you’ve configured on the Deck. You can also map custom layouts in Settings.

03 / CLIPBOARD

Share plain text

Copy text from your Mac to the Deck, from the Deck to your Mac, or both. Clipboard sharing requires an active connection and a Wayland session in Desktop Mode. Each direction is off by default and must be enabled on both devices.

IN REAL LIFE

Using MacDeck at your desk

Set up at your desk

Dock your Deck, open a browser, and type with the keyboard already on your Mac.

Switch when you need to type

Use a mouse or the Deck’s trackpad to select a field, then switch the keyboard over to type.

Share text between devices

Copy a URL or command on your Mac and paste it on the Deck once clipboard sharing is enabled.

GET STARTED / 02

Set up MacDeck

Download both apps from the same release and connect your Mac and Steam Deck to the same trusted local network. The setup guide has the installer and permission commands for the Deck.

Read the setup guide ↗
  1. 01

    Install on your Mac

    Unzip the Mac app and move it to Applications. On first launch, approve it with Open Anyway in Privacy & Security, then grant Accessibility access so MacDeck can read your keystrokes.

  2. 02

    Install on Steam Deck

    Switch to Desktop Mode, extract the Deck download, and run the installer in Konsole. Complete the one-time virtual keyboard permission setup, then log out and back in.

  3. 03

    Pair and switch

    Open MacDeck on the Deck and copy the IP address and pairing secret from Settings into the Mac app’s Settings. On the Deck, open Control and select Enable Mac Input. Press Control–Shift–F12 on the Mac to start typing on the Deck.

A NOTE ON PRIVACY / 03

Encrypted on your local network

The connection is authenticated and encrypted. Typed text and clipboard contents are not logged or stored. Use MacDeck on a trusted network and keep your pairing secret private.
